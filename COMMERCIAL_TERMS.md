## 💼 Available Commercial Models

We offer flexible acquisition and licensing structures tailored to M&A brokers, technology buyers, enterprise software providers, and Web3/HealthTech platforms.

### 1. Outright IP Transfer (Full Buyout)
* **Description:** Complete transfer of ownership, copyright, patent rights, trademark applications, and intellectual property of the source code, design assets, and underlying algorithms.
* **Includes:**
  * Exclusive ownership of **Perceptio Neuro Labs** (BCI / EEG Engine) and **IrisVerify & IrisVault** (Biometric Protocols).
  * Full source code repository history and continuous developer handoff support (up to 40 hours of technical integration consulting).
  * Transfer of all design artifacts, vector assets, and UI components.
  * Supplementary operational assets (SIT v2.0, Kobalt, Inkanto, ClusterLaunch AWS Kit).
* **Ideal for:** Private Equity, strategic competitors, or tech conglomerates looking to integrate high-value biometric/neural tech into their core products.

### 2. Exclusive / Non-Exclusive Source Code License
* **Description:** A commercial, perpetual, royalty-free license to use, modify, sub-license, and distribute the software embedded within your commercial products.
* **Options:**
  * **Exclusive License:** Your company is the sole licensed commercial operator of this technology in your industry/region.
  * **Non-Exclusive License:** Perpetual right to deploy and modify the source code internally or within client systems without exclusivity.
* **Ideal for:** Systems integrators, defense/healthtech contractors, and digital identity platforms.

### 3. B2B Module & SaaS Integration License
* **Description:** A custom, API-based or self-hosted containerized licensing option where we assist in integrating our low-latency neural processing pipeline or webcam-based biometric scanning features directly into your existing infrastructure.
* **Ideal for:** Mid-market SaaS platforms seeking rapid feature expansion with minimal overhead.
